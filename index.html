    This vs That live

    Creator & developer · 2025 – now

    An AI judge that’s allowed to say “it depends”.

    built
    A 5-stage pipeline on top of Stanford’s Co-STORM model. It contains fixed rules to pick a verdict, and is not a standalone model.
    hard part
    My test cases and actual user inputs had a difference in accuracy rates. To fix this, I found a filter that was faulty which threw away a quarter of the conflicts, so I deleted it.
    learned
    In general, AI is great at facts but horrible with judgement. On complex real-world questions, it’s unable to match any perspective that a sentient human brings.

    ##research

    Author · Oxford Journal of Student Scholarship · Oct 2026

    Perceptions of AI Voice Explanations for Medical Reports

    Conducted a field study for MediTrack on whether people would like to hear their medical records being explained in their own language.

    method
    Surveyed 103 adults and conducted 9 interviews in a rural hospital in Muzaffarnagar.
    findings
    99 of the 103 preferred voice, and 81.6% of them struggled with the jargon and language in their reports, often relying on a doctor to clarify their doubts.
    learned
    Voice is the best method for communicating with unfamiliar technology. Using text becomes unintuitive when voice options exist.
